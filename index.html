Antoine Kempf

As an EUMaster4HPC alumni, I am conducting my PhD research at CEA on the multi-GPU optimization of the neutron transport code DONUT. My work focuses on adapting transport sweep algorithms and diffusion solvers for distributed architectures using Kokkos and MPI. The main objective is to mitigate communication latency to improve the scalability of reactor simulations.
